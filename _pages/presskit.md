### Short Bio
I code & build AI/biotech research teams

### Long Bio
I am a Computational Biologist who's passionate about reinventing healthcare. As a strategy consultant, I've worked across a variety of industries from automotive to healthcare and everything in between. I love working with every size business from startups to enterprise companies on projects that include data analysis, ML pipeline design, and VR/AR experience creation. In my most recent experience as a Director of Research, I enjoyed building up the department and leading R&D on digital instruments, and managing internal programs for AI tooling. I take pride in guiding medical device innovators through SBIR and FDA submissions, ensuring their groundbreaking ideas reach the market successfully. For early-stage founders, I provide the tools and knowledge to enhance their overall strategy and connect with their customer base, paving the way for more precise product development.
